# School management and e-learning strategy study plan

Date: 21 September 2026. Status: desk research complete; documentation links, whitespace and illustrative arithmetic verified. Recommendations remain proposed. Research and documentation only, requested by the owner; application implementation is outside this task.

## Scope

Evaluate Nova Scholar as a management platform for Kenyan day, boarding and mixed schools, with school-provided e-learning and affordable individual learning subscriptions. Compare this direction with the two existing studies and assess buyer needs, competition, access packages, economics, delivery risk, project reuse and a practical validation sequence.

The owner clarified during research that the small learning subscription is for students at non-subscribing schools. Use school-funded administration plus included learning for customer schools, and independent family-funded learning for other learners. Address overlapping payments when school cover begins.

## Dependencies and method

- Read the documentation index, requirements, implementation roadmap, both existing studies and their research plan, architecture, data model, decisions and implementation records.
- Inspect the repository's module inventory to distinguish available foundations from new school capabilities. This is not a code audit or a fresh verification of application behavior.
- Research current official vendor, curriculum, payment and privacy sources. Separate published claims, project evidence, analytical conclusions and untested proposals. Disclose inaccessible sources and pricing inconsistencies.
- Preserve existing work and accepted scope. Link the new research from the index and roadmap; retain earlier studies as dated analyses.

## Acceptance criteria

- Give a reasoned recommendation and explain what changes from the earlier studies.
- Specify common administration needs and distinct day/boarding workflows, buyers and operating responsibilities.
- Define school-included learning and independent learner subscriptions, including expiry, transfers, privacy and duplicate-purchase boundaries.
- Compare relevant school-management/learning competitors using attributable evidence, not assumed market share.
- Provide proposed packaging, transparent scenario arithmetic, acquisition/support costs, seasonality and affordability limits.
- Identify conceptual architecture/data boundaries and gaps against the current project without implementing speculative tables or changing requirements.
- Recommend a focused initial segment, staged delivery gates, discovery questions and measurable pilot decisions.

## Verification

Review source support and research limits; recalculate numerical examples; check local Markdown links and documentation whitespace. No application tests or live transactions are needed for this documentation-only study. Record actual checks in the implementation log.

## Unresolved decisions

Reachable school partners and their budgets; initial grade/subject; content rights and reviewers; delivery team and support capacity; child-data arrangements; school payment-provider access; scope approval following study review. Proposed prices, budgets and pilot thresholds are experiments, not commitments.
